Baseline — Quick Start

Basketball Practice Planner · 151 drills with moving diagrams.
From “what do we do today?” to a printed plan in six steps.

Scan to opendecentschott.github.io
/gym-builder
1

Set up your gym

Tap the hoop button at the top. Pick court space, hoops, players and level. The button sets team colors and diagram style.

2

Find drills

Search or tap a category. Fits my gym hides drills you can't run. ★ My playbook = your own drills. Tap + to add one to your plan.

3

Watch it move

Tap any drill — the lines draw, then players move. Use ‹ › to go step by step. Read "Tell your players" out loud.

4

Build a practice

Open Practice Plan. Pick a length and up to 3 focus areas, then tap Build my practice — or start from a template.

5

Make it yours

Drag the ⠿ handle to reorder · ↻ swap · − + minutes · ✕ remove. Set a start time for real clock times. Save keeps it.

6

Print or share

Print view makes a clipboard sheet with step diagrams. Copy as text to paste it into a group text with your staff.

Coach tips

  • Install it: on your phone, Share → Add to Home Screen. Works with no Wi-Fi after the first visit.
  • Every drill has easier/harder versions and coaching cues to yell.
  • Add your own drills: send a link, text or a PDF plan to Ron — they come back with diagrams.
